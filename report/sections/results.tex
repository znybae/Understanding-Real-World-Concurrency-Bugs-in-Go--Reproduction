\section{Results}

\subsection{Official Week~2 reproduction run}
On 14 Nov 2026, Block~1 ran \texttt{scripts/run\_all.sh} with \texttt{REPEATS=3}
inside the shared Go 1.10.8 linux/amd64 Docker image. All 18 checks (six cases
$\times$ three runs) matched the expected checker outcome. For the binary
indicator that a run matched its expected outcome, every case has a mean of
$1.00$ and a sample standard deviation of $0.00$. These statistics describe
only that indicator; they are not run times and are not the paper's aggregate
detection rates.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \caption{Official Week~2 outcomes (mean $\pm$ sample std of the
    expected-outcome indicator over three runs).}
  \label{tab:week2-outcomes}
  \begin{tabular}{llccc}
    \toprule
    Case & Version & Expected result & Matches & Indicator mean $\pm$ std \\
    \midrule
    Kubernetes \#5316 & buggy & EXPECTED\_BLOCKING & 3/3 & $1.00 \pm 0.00$ \\
    Docker \#24007    & buggy & EXPECTED\_PANIC    & 3/3 & $1.00 \pm 0.00$ \\
    Docker \#25384    & buggy & EXPECTED\_BLOCKING & 3/3 & $1.00 \pm 0.00$ \\
    Docker \#25384    & fixed & FIXED\_PASS        & 3/3 & $1.00 \pm 0.00$ \\
    Docker \#27037    & buggy & EXPECTED\_RACE     & 3/3 & $1.00 \pm 0.00$ \\
    Docker \#27037    & fixed & FIXED\_PASS        & 3/3 & $1.00 \pm 0.00$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The two blocking cases were exposed by the \texttt{go test} timeout, not by
Go's runtime deadlock detector: none of the 18 logs contain
\texttt{all goroutines are asleep - deadlock}. In the Figure~8 buggy runs the
race detector fired every time, and the printed versions contained duplicates
(for example \texttt{v1.19 v1.21 v1.21 v1.21}); the fixed version printed
\texttt{v1.17} to \texttt{v1.20} once each in every run.

\subsection{Traceability}
The official Week~2 evidence directory is
\texttt{report/results/week2\_full\_suite/run-20261114T075540Z-JMQOiG/}.
It contains \texttt{environment.txt}, \texttt{summary.tsv},
\texttt{statistics.csv}, \texttt{input\_sha256.txt} and one \texttt{.log} file
per case and iteration. Every number in \Cref{tab:week2-outcomes} and
\Cref{tab:test-record} comes from that directory. The run recorded base commit
\texttt{312d6da}, together with the uncommitted integration scripts that were
merged later as pull request \#5 (\texttt{68df8a3}).
