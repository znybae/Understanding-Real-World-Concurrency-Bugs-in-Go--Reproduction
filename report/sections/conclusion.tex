\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{Overall Verdict}
This study \emph{partially reproduced} Tu et al.~\cite{tu2019go}: the six
selected kernels reproduced their expected symptoms under the official
Go 1.10.8 setup, but claims C1--C3 remain inconclusive at the paper's
aggregate, distinct-bug scale, and the Figure~8 race disappears under the
Go 1.22 per-iteration language rule rather than because a newer toolchain
is used by itself.

\subsection{Lessons Learned}
A pinned container image is not enough if the language version and the
toolchain are allowed to change together. In the extension, Go 1.23.12 still
exhibited the Figure~8 race when \texttt{go.mod} selected the old rule, and
the race disappeared only after that one language-version setting was
changed. Selected kernels also cannot stand in for the paper's benchmark
counts: our official runs establish repeatable mechanisms, not the original
proportions over distinct bugs. Finally, a test timeout is not a runtime
deadlock diagnosis. None of the official Week~2 or extension logs contained
\texttt{all goroutines are asleep - deadlock}, so detector claims have to be
scored against the detector's own message, not against harness timeouts.
